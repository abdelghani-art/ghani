About me

Visuals that tell a story

I’m Abdelghani Boulaamane, a graphic designer and visual artist. I create photo manipulations, packaging and menus, social media content, event posters and YouTube thumbnails, working in French, Arabic and English.

From student organisations and conferences to startups, cafés and clinics, I like to turn every brief into a bold, scroll-stopping image.
